How to Play SkyWing

1. Choose Fun Mode (FC) or Real Mode (PKR)

Use the top switcher anytime. Fun Credits are free virtual points for practice; Real Mode uses your Pakistani Rupee balance funded via EasyPaisa, JazzCash, or Bank.

2. Place One or Two Side-by-Side Bets

During the 5-second countdown, place bets on Bet 1 and/or Bet 2. Auto Cashout is OFF by default — turn it ON only if you want automatic cashout at a target multiplier.

3. Cash Out Before the Bird Flies Away

Watch the multiplier climb from 1.00x. Tap Cash Out before the bird bursts to lock in your winnings.
